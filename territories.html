Territory Questions, Answered

What exactly counts as a "territory"?+
A territory is one UK postcode area — the letters at the start of a postcode, like BS for Bristol or LS for Leeds. Bigger cities can be split further on request during your audit call.
Why three trades per territory, not one?+
We cap each postcode at three businesses and never take two of the same trade. So an electrician, a builder and a solar installer can share an area — but you'll never compete with another electrician we work with.
What happens if my area is already taken?+
Join the waitlist from the area's popup. If a spot opens up, waitlisted trades are offered it first, in the order they joined.
Does reserving an area commit me to anything?+
No. A reservation just holds your spot while we run your free audit. Your territory is only locked once you sign up to a package.
